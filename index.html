About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Computing at Dublin City University (DCU).

My research focuses on connected health, healthcare information systems, artificial intelligence, multimodal data analytics and intelligent healthcare systems.

Research Areas

  • Connected Health
  • Healthcare Artificial Intelligence
  • Medical Informatics
  • Multimodal AI
  • Applied Analytics
  • Digital Health
  • Healthcare Data Integration

Current Research Projects

Graph-Augmented Healthcare Information Architecture

Research on multi-centre healthcare data integration, semantic alignment, knowledge graphs and intelligent clinical analytics.

Multimodal AI for Healthcare

Combining clinical, imaging, speech and other healthcare modalities for intelligent diagnosis and decision support.

Connected Health and Lifelogging

Intelligent analysis of multimodal lifelog data for activity recognition, retrieval and healthcare applications.
